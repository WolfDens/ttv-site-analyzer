Site Analyzer — v2 redesign

A design concept for the underwriting tool, split into two purpose-built surfaces instead of one seven-step form. Built in response to the team huddle feedback: the current tool is “information overload,” and the output can’t be tweaked on the fly.

Read this before testing. These are UX prototypes. The numbers recompute live so the screens feel real, but the arithmetic is a simplified illustrative model — it is not the production underwriting engine. Wiring these to the real math is a later, separate step. Judge the layout, flow, and feel, not the dollar figures. The live production app is untouched and still running from main.

What to poke at

  • Site: drag the building footprint around inside the envelope; hit rotate; flip the corner-lot switch.
  • Site: change units — watch the townhome row length check itself against Charlotte’s 150 ft max building length.
  • Site: open “Adjust dimensions & setbacks” — every number is still there, just demoted behind one panel.
  • Underwrite: drag the land-cost lever and watch all three scenarios move together.
  • Underwrite: edit Retail $/sf inline, then open “Advanced” to see what was hidden.
  • Both: does it feel calmer than today’s tool? That’s the whole question.
